# Awesome Quit Nicotine [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of free and useful resources for quitting nicotine: pouches such as Zyn, Velo and On!, snus, vapes and cigarettes. Apps, guides, calculators, helplines and communities.

Maintained by the maker of [PouchWise](https://pouchwiseapp.com/). Other apps are listed on merit, including direct competitors. This list is not medical advice; talk to a doctor or pharmacist about nicotine replacement and other treatment. Pull requests are welcome, see [Contributing](#contributing).

## Apps for Nicotine Pouches and Snus

- [PouchWise](https://pouchwiseapp.com/) - iPhone app that tapers you off pouches with a daily allowance that steps down to a quit date.
- [Pouch Buddy](https://pouchbuddy.app) - Pouch tracker with a daily limit and cutting back with friends, on iPhone and Android.
- [Pouched](https://www.pouched.app) - Usage tracking, taper plans and cost savings for Zyn, Velo and On! users.
- [QuitNic](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6745766195) - iPhone app for quitting Zyn and smoking.
- [NiQuit](https://www.niquit.app) - Free course, coach and quit tracker for smoking and vaping, with pouches and snus covered too.

## Apps for Smoking and Vaping

- [quitSTART](https://smokefree.gov/tools-tips/apps/quitstart) - Free app from the US National Cancer Institute's Smokefree.gov.
- [Smoke Free](https://smokefreeapp.com) - Long-running quit smoking app built around evidence-based techniques.
- [Kwit](https://kwit.app) - Quit smoking app with progress tracking, on iOS and Android.
- [QuitSure](https://quitsure.app) - Quit smoking programme delivered through an app.

## Helplines and Official Programmes

- [Smokefree.gov](https://smokefree.gov) - US government quit resources, including text programmes.
- [1-800-QUIT-NOW](https://1800quitnow.cancer.gov) - Free US quitline that connects you to your state's service.
- [NHS Quit Smoking](https://www.nhs.uk/better-health/quit-smoking/) - Free UK support, including local stop smoking services.
